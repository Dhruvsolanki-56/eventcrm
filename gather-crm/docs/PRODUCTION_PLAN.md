# Gather CRM production build plan

## Product contract

Gather is one CRM with two isolated workspace types: an exhibitor company with admin, manager and representative roles, and an attendee's private space. A user who belongs to both can switch spaces. The active space is always visible. The first screen after setup is Capture.

Capture is the primary journey: point the camera at a card, brochure or QR code, or choose images from the device; each shutter photo or selected image is resized and uploaded immediately; upload immediately creates a scan and starts background reading. On phones, the single-photo `Take photo` picker uses `capture="environment"` separately from the multi-image gallery picker, and both feed the same immediate upload path. The camera stays ready for the next image. The user opens the ready tray, checks one item at a time, saves, and optionally approves an email. There is no extra Scan or Start reading button after upload. The user can always enter details manually if reading fails or no AI key is configured. QR detection runs on the device and does not require a separate badge screen.

Only Name, Company, Email, Phone, Job title and Website appear as contact fields in review. Name plus email or phone is required to save. Quality, products of interest, note, voice note and follow-up date are compact optional additions. No email is sent before the user reviews and presses Send. No screen may claim a card was read, a voice note transcribed, or an email delivered without corresponding evidence.

After “Save person,” each user can choose “Ask every time” (default) or “Never ask.” The choice is private to that user in that workspace. “Save & email” remains an explicit request to open the suggested draft; “Save & scan next” keeps the rapid-capture path moving without opening a draft.

Every contact belongs to exactly one company inside its workspace. One company has many contacts, each with its own encounters, notes, products of interest, emails, meetings and follow-ups. A deal value belongs to the company, so reports count it once. Saving the same scan again is idempotent. The company matching order is normalized website or business email domain, normalized name, then a similar-name confirmation. A matching email or phone offers a same-person decision; linking creates a new encounter, not another contact.

## Required implementation

- Frontend: React, Vite, TypeScript, React Router, plain CSS variables, Recharts, Lucide, locally packaged Fraunces and Inter fonts. The desktop has a sidebar and the phone has a bottom tab bar and thumb-reachable Capture action. Review remains one screen.
- Backend: Express and TypeScript, REST routes under `/api`, Zod validation, one authorization-aware SQLite access layer using `better-sqlite3`, checked-in SQL migrations, and an in-process durable jobs worker with leases, bounded retry and backoff.
- Data: the tables and ownership rules from the supplied capture-first specification, with foreign keys, uniqueness constraints, contact versions, UTC timestamps, event time zones, integer minor-unit money, archive versus delete, and authorized media storage outside public assets. Saved person details can be edited with a version check; stale writes are refused and the user can reload the latest record.
- Identity: email/password with argon2, server-side sessions, opaque server-validated Secure HttpOnly SameSite cookies under HTTPS, CSRF protection on changes, rate limits, invite expiry and revocation, role/event checks on every endpoint, and immediate loss of access after member removal.
- Providers: Anthropic for image reading and optional drafts and follow-up suggestions; SMTP through Nodemailer; optional speech-to-text adapter with no provider selected. With no key, reading becomes manual review, drafts use a simple template, mail becomes an explicitly unsent outbox, and voice notes remain playable with user-entered text.
- Safety: validate uploaded size, MIME and bytes; treat card, note and transcript content as untrusted data; schema-check AI output; never allow AI output to perform actions; use parameterized SQL and escaped UI output; provide signed unsubscribe and enforce do-not-contact.
- Operations: one Node service on a VM with a local persistent disk for SQLite and uploads, TLS at the reverse proxy, WAL mode, a single active writer/worker, health checks, structured logs, monitored failed jobs, online database backups plus media backups, and a tested restore procedure. Do not place SQLite WAL on a network filesystem. See [SQLite WAL](https://www.sqlite.org/wal.html) and [online backup](https://www.sqlite.org/backup.html).

## Build sequence and gates

1. **Design system and shell:** scaffold the project, migrations, seed data, sign-in and onboarding, role-aware navigation, workspace strip, Home, Settings skeleton, job worker and provider fallbacks. Run the app and check every visible action at 390 px and 1440 px. Prove two exhibitor companies and two attendees cannot read each other's data; verify a failed job appears in Problems and Retry works.
2. **Capture:** camera, photo picker, gallery batch intake, immediate upload and reading, QR detection, status tray, one-at-a-time review, uncertainty labels, company and person matching, idempotent save and Save & scan next. Upload five sample images quickly; verify review order, one company for multiple people and one contact for repeat scans.
3. **Email and voice:** editable suggested email sheet, explicit Send, queue and outbox states, failure Retry, signed unsubscribe, quick two-minute recording, authorized playback and manual text. Verify an edited draft is exactly what SMTP receives; a voice note is attached only to its contact.
4. **Lead work:** person and company pages, timelines, inline actions, stage changes, follow-ups, meetings and overlap warnings, reminders and digest, pipeline, reports, CSV export and audit log. Walk the complete packaging-buyer story to a won deal and confirm company value is counted once.
5. **Attendee mode:** reuse capture and contact work in the private workspace with About me, private follow-ups, export and deletion. Verify attendee content is absent from exhibitor pages, search, API responses and exports.
6. **Production audit:** automated full journeys and route/button crawl in both modes, forced provider/job failures, permission and concurrency tests, keyboard and responsive checks, real scan timing samples, staging deployment rehearsal, backup/restore drill, and review of logs for sensitive data. Fix failures before release.

Each gate requires a running app, phone and desktop browser checks, working buttons and links, passing relevant automated tests, and a recorded result before advancing. A failed or unverified gate stays open.

## Release criteria

The release must pass the original specification's explicit tests for workspace isolation, removed-member access, signup email verification, password reset/session revocation, repeat scans, company deduplication, same-person encounters, exact email drafts, mail retry, unsubscribe, voice ownership and deletion, report totals, attendee isolation, full scan-to-email and attendee browser journeys, and the route/button crawl. It must also pass a staging restart, migration, backup and restore exercise on the chosen single-VM topology. Record actual capture timings on sample images and, when configured, live provider calls separately; never present fixture timings as live AI performance.

The README must list exact setup, seed, development, test, build, start, backup and restore commands; environment variables; phone-camera requirements; screenshots; measured results; and remaining operational limits. Production readiness is established by these checks and deployment evidence, not by a blanket security claim. An independent security review is required before describing the system as security-audited.
